Bolt Circle (PCD) Hole Pattern & CNC G-code Guide

1. Polar to Cartesian Coordinate Calculations

For a bolt circle of diameter PCD, total hole count N, and starting angle α0, each hole coordinate (i = 0 to N - 1) is:

Xi = Xc + (PCD / 2) × cos(α0 + i × (360° / N))

Yi = Yc + (PCD / 2) × sin(α0 + i × (360° / N))

2. Using Coordinates with CNC Canned Cycles
