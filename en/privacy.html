Privacy Policy & Cookie Information

Information on data protection (GDPR), browser localStorage, and advertising cookies

1. General Information

CNCHelper is a free engineering calculator toolkit and reference database for CNC operators, setters, and programmers. Using the site requires no registration or personal account.

2. Browser Local Storage (localStorage)

For convenience, this site stores only technical preferences in your browser's localStorage: selected language (ru/pl/en), UI theme (light/dark), cookie consent status, and calculations you explicitly save using the 'Save calculation' button. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared anytime in browser settings.

3. Cookies & Advertising (Google AdSense)

This website uses Google AdSense. With your consent (via the cookie banner), Google and its partners may use cookies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time via the 'Cookie settings' link in the footer.

4. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please contact the site administrator via email at Exttemeart@gmail.com.
