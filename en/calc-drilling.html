CNC Drilling, Drill Point Cone & Spot Drill Guide

1. Drilling Speeds and Feeds

Spindle RPM and feed rate calculations for drills:

n = (1000 × Vc) / (π × D) [RPM]  |  F = n × f [mm/min]

2. Drill Point Cone Length lp for Blind Holes

Blueprints specify cylindrical depth Hcyl. For the full diameter to reach that depth, the Z-axis must travel to Z = Hcyl + lp:

lp = (D / 2) / tan(σ / 2)

  • Standard HSS drills (σ = 118°): lp ≈ 0.300 × D
  • Solid carbide drills (σ = 140°): lp ≈ 0.182 × D

3. Chamfer Depth & Spot Drill Z Plunge

Step depth for countersinking: h = (D_chamfer - D_hole) / (2 × tan(α / 2)).

Full plunge from sharp tip of a 90° Spot Drill: Z_spot = D_chamfer / (2 × tan(α / 2)) (for 90° tool: Z_spot = D_chamfer / 2).
