CNC Milling Theory: Speeds, Feeds, RCT & Spindle Power

1. Spindle Speed and Table Feed Rate

Primary cutting data formulas for end mills and face mills:

n = (1000 × Vc) / (π × D) [RPM]  |  F = n × fz × z [mm/min]

2. Radial Chip Thinning (RCT) Adjustment

In climb milling with low radial engagement (ae < 0.5 × D, trochoidal or dynamic milling), the actual chip thickness hex is significantly thinner than the programmed feed per tooth fz. Without adjustment, the tool rubs and work-hardens the workpiece:

fz_adj = fz / (2 × √((ae / D) × (1 - ae / D)))

Example: A D = 12 mm end mill taking ae = 2 mm radial stepover allows boosting programmed fz from 0.05 mm/tooth to 0.067 mm/tooth (+34%), achieving higher MRR without overstressing cutting edges.

3. Net Cutting Power Pc & Spindle Motor Power Pm

  • Material Removal Rate (MRR): Q = (ap × ae × vf) / 1000 [cm³/min]
  • Net Cutting Power (Pc): Pc = (ap × ae × vf × kc) / (60 × 10⁶) [kW]
  • Spindle Motor Power (Pm): Pm = Pc / η [kW] (drive efficiency η ≈ 0.85)
  • Spindle Torque (Mc): Mc = (9550 × Pc) / n [N·m]
