Metric Thread Sizing & Tap Drill Guide

1. Tap Drill Sizing for Cutting Taps

Standard cutting tap drill diameter formula:

D_drill = D - P (where D is nominal major diameter, P is pitch in mm)

Examples: M6×1 → drill Ø5.0 mm | M8×1.25 → drill Ø6.8 mm | M10×1.5 → drill Ø8.5 mm | M12×1.75 → drill Ø10.2 mm.

2. Hole Size for Cold Forming / Roll Taps

Forming taps displace material plastically without creating chips. The required hole is significantly larger:

D_form = D - (P / 2) × 0.9 = D - 0.45 × P

Critical Rule: For M10×1.5, a roll tap requires a Ø9.3 mm hole. Using an 8.5 mm drill will instantly snap the forming tap due to extreme torque.

3. Minor Diameter and Thread Profile Depth

  • Internal thread minor diameter (D1): D1 = D - 1.0825 × P
  • Thread profile engagement depth (H1): H1 = 0.6134 × P
