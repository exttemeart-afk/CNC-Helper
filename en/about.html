About CNCHelper & Contact

Practical shop-floor CNC toolkit — fast, free, and no sign-up required

Why CNCHelper Exists

CNCHelper is built as a practical shop-floor companion for CNC machinists, setup technicians, and CAM programmers: turning and milling speeds & feeds, Ra/Rz surface finish, spindle power, ISO 286 fits, threads & 3-wire measurement, and a searchable 260+ G & M code reference (Fanuc, Haas, Siemens, Heidenhain, Mazak, Okuma).

All calculations run locally in your browser so the tool stays fast even on weak shop-floor Wi-Fi. If you spot a formula discrepancy or want to suggest a new calculator, G-code, or article, please reach out.
